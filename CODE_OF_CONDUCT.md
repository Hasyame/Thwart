# Code of conduct

This is a small project about a card game. The whole of it is: **be decent to
each other.**

What that means in practice:

- Assume the other person is doing their best. Most misunderstandings here are
  language, not malice. Contributors come from several countries and many are
  writing in their second language.
- Nobody is expected to know how GitHub works, how Android builds, or how open
  source is normally done. "I do not understand" is always an acceptable thing
  to say, and never something to be embarrassed about.
- Criticise the work, never the person. "This step contradicts page 12 of the
  booklet" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Do not push. I maintain this in my spare time, around a job and a life. An
  unanswered issue is not disrespect.

Not welcome, and dealt with without discussion: harassment, insults, personal
attacks, sexual attention or imagery, sharing someone's private information, and
persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Email **marvelchampcompanion@proton.me**. It reaches me alone.

You do not need proof, and you do not have to have handled it perfectly
yourself. Tell me what happened.

I will read it, reply, and take it seriously. Depending on what happened I may
ask someone to change their behaviour, edit or remove what they wrote, or block
them from the project. I am the only maintainer, so that decision is mine, and I
will explain it to the people involved rather than acting silently.
