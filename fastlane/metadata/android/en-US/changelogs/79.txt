Fear No Evil, checked against the English rulebook.

The scenarios, villains, environments and cards now carry their official
English titles: Art Museum Heist, The Getaway, Protection Racket, The Raft
Breakout, Stop the Presses!, Kingpin; Purple Man, Typhoid Mary, Establish
Trust, Disturbed Psyche, Master Key, and the rest.

Three rules were wrong. Losing a game does not fail a scenario: it stays
open, against the same Underling, until the pressure reaches three (and on
Expert, a defeat adds one mark). On an Expert campaign, a defeat against
Kingpin costs one Completed environment, flipped to Failed, and with none
left the campaign is lost. And on Expert the app now asks each player's
remaining hit points after every game, won or lost, as the log requires.

Also: The Getaway reveals the Jackknifed Tanker Truck from one mark; and the
recommended villain order draws which of Purple Man and Typhoid Mary comes
fourth, as the book leaves that open.

Underneath, a lost scenario with questions on its page (victory points,
hit points, the finale's sacrifice) now asks them; before, every defeat
was filed unasked, in every campaign.
