Fear No Evil in English, and it asks how you want to play it.

The campaign is translated. The story, the setup steps, the questions and the
notes on each subordinate are all in English now. Card names stay French: this
pack is not on MarvelCDB, so an English title would be a guess, and a guess
printed next to the card in your hand is worse than the French name. The app
says as much before you start.

That uncovered a wider bug. Everything a campaign said was being shown in the
card language rather than the app language, so an English app with French cards
read all six campaigns in French. Campaign text now follows the app.

Before a Fear No Evil campaign starts, the app asks whether you want the villain
order the book recommends for a first campaign, or a random one. The subordinate
for a mission is now chosen as you pick that mission, and the name sticks, so
retrying a mission faces the same villain.

Each way out of a defeat says what it costs: retry, continue and fail the
mission, take a break, or stop the campaign and keep the stats. The pre-setup
also names the environment card for the mission you are about to play, since you
turn it over the moment the scenario ends.

The web companion is gone. It did nothing the app does not.

The bundled card data is smaller. MarvelCDB sends each card's linked card nested
inside it as well as separately, and the app never read the nested copy, so it is
dropped as the data is fetched. That is 1.6 MB less to read on a first run.
