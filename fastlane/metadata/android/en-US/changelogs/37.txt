Fixes a crash when choosing a scenario.

1.21.0 set ACHIEVED and FAILED apart in bold italic wherever they appear, and
the pattern that finds them used a flag that only exists on the JVM. Android
matches with a different regex engine, which rejected it — so the app crashed
the first time it drew any campaign text, which is the moment you pick a
scenario.

The unit tests run on the JVM and were perfectly happy, which is why this got
out. They now assert the pattern carries no such flag, and that an accented
keyword is actually found.
