Fixes from real games, and the Fear No Evil tracker.

The health and threat tracker now works in Fear No Evil. It never could:
MarvelCDB has entered 68 of that pack's 276 cards and none of its villains,
so there was nothing to count. The numbers are now carried by the campaign
itself, read off the cards.

Also fixed:

- Standard III showed as "Standard_iii" in your statistics.
- Two-handed solo was logged to BoardGameGeek as two players. Only you are
  sent now, however many decks you hold; the heroes are named in the note.
- Packs curated in an update reached only fresh installs, so Jessica Jones
  and Luke Cage stayed in wave 0 for anyone who already had the app.
- Two Fear No Evil questions about Mary Typhoide were wrong.

New in statistics: sort a table worst-first, and measure a box by losses
instead of wins.
