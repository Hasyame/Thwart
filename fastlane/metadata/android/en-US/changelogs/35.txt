The ambient music links are gone.

The play screen had a Music button that opened a preconfigured Spotify
playlist, and Settings had a field for your own playlist link plus a shortcut
to Melodice. All of it has been removed: the button, the setting, and the
stored link.

Shipping those links preconfigured meant a libre app pointing you at non-libre
services out of the box, which F-Droid quite reasonably flags. The feature only
ever opened a URL in another app — you can do that from your music app
directly, and it will keep playing over the timer exactly as before. It was not
worth carrying an anti-feature label for.

Nothing else changes. The app still talks to MarvelCDB for card data, and to
BoardGameGeek only if you set that up yourself.
