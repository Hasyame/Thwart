Achievements.

Everything you have beaten, with whom, and what to play next, read from
your game history: a heroes x scenarios grid with its filters, and
thirty-one named achievements (box coverage, difficulty, volume, table
size, campaigns, draft). The home page shows your count, the latest one
earned and your badges; a game's result page says what it unlocked.

Backups move to format 2: the owner's seat and the game mode are
recorded, and fields from a newer app are kept rather than dropped.
