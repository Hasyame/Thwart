A game picked up after a long break carries on where it stood.

Resuming used to start it again from nothing. The clock went back to zero,
losing however long you had already played, and the tracker came back at
full health with the main scheme empty, because the threat and the round
were never saved at all.

Both are fixed. The app now writes down where the counters stood itself,
rather than asking you to copy numbers it already has on screen, and the
clock carries on from where you stopped it.

Also in campaigns: the clock can be corrected by tapping it, the same as
in a one-off game, and when the tracker cannot count a scenario the page
now says why instead of showing nothing.
