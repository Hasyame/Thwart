The Stats page is easier to read.

Each table now sits in its own box you can open and close, and a single
search field filters all of them at once. A box opens to its first few
rows rather than all of them, with a button to see the rest.

Every bar says what it measures, and you can switch between wins, games
played and time spent. Tables can be sorted alphabetically, by most
played, or by best rate.

The table headings were dark ink on bright red and hard to read. Fixed.
