If your confirmation email never arrived, you can ask for another.

An account whose address is not confirmed yet now says so on the account
screen, explains that the account is switched off until you follow the link,
and offers to send the link again. It asks for your password, because that is
the only request an unconfirmed account is allowed to make.

Two things the app used to say were no longer true and are fixed. The
registration form said your address was never verified and no message was sent
to it; it is, and one is. The recovery-code screen said there was no email
address on the account, moments after you had typed one.

And there is now a way to support the work, at the bottom of Settings. Thwart
is free and stays free: nothing is behind it, nothing is withheld without it,
and it is mentioned nowhere else.
