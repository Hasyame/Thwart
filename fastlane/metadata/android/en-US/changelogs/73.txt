Thank you Dani, whose email this release is.

You can rename a deck now, from the menu on the deck's own screen. Imported
decks too, which is the point: MarvelCDB names a deck after its hero unless
its author renamed it, so a list of imports can be four decks all called
Iron Man.

Deleting a deck asks first. The bin sat next to the deck you meant to open.

A name you typed while the app was loading a hero could be thrown away and
replaced by the hero's name. It is yours the moment you touch the field.

A row of chips that ran onto a second line had no gap above it, which is
what made Standard III look wrong. Fixed everywhere it happens.

In a decklist the type mark now takes the colour of the card's aspect: the
shape was already telling you the type. And the same mark is on the deck
view, which had none.

Starting a campaign asks about difficulty once instead of twice.
