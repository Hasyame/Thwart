La Poursuite accelerates by two threat per player a round, not one. The
museum prints a star instead of a number: its acceleration is the number
of ART attachments on the villain plus one, which the app cannot see, so
it adds nothing at the end of the round and the briefing says to add it
yourself. A tracker that guesses is worse than one that says it cannot
know.

Le Caid turns over. Each difficulty plays one card with two sides: 25
health per player on standard, 28 on expert, unchanged when he flips.
