Civil War, played the way it was meant to be: two teams against each other.

Competitive mode is on the Play tab. Each team builds a scenario and hands it
to the other, so the app asks what each side is facing: the enemy's leader, and
the enemy's pick of main schemes out of the four their side offers. Then both
boards sit on one screen, because the question that mode asks is which side is
further along, and that cannot be answered while each half is on a different
phone.

Ending a round advances both boards together. The rulebook keeps the two sides
in step, and a game does not end until both have played the same number of
phases. If it comes down to a tie, the five tie-breaks are listed in the order
you apply them.

Civil War also works properly in ordinary play now. It puts a leader where every
other box puts a villain, and the app was only looking for villains, so counting
health in a Civil War game found nothing at all. Leaders count now.

Campaigns you do not own the box for are no longer offered. Choosing one only
led to a setup step naming cards you do not have.

NeXt Evolution draws two more things the book leaves to chance: the order the
seven Marauders arrive in, and which of the three Sinister Experiments is
removed from the game.
