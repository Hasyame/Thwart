Any game you played on your own or from a draw can be played again.

Each of them has a play button in the history, under Stats. It opens the
setup page with that game already on it: scenario, difficulty and the
Standard set an Expert game was played with, the modular sets, and every
seat, matched back to the deck it came from when you still have it. Change
what you like and start.

Not for a campaign's scenario: a campaign is played again from its own box.

For that, the modular sets are now kept by code with each game as well as
by name in its notes. Games from before this are read back by name, which
works when the cards are in the language they were in that day.
