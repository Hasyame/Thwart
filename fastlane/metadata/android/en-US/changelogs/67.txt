Your collection, decks, campaigns and play history can follow you between
devices.

It stays off until you ask for it. Creating an account and turning sync on
are two separate steps, and the second one tells you what merging would
mean, in plays and decks and campaigns, before anything at all is written.
The answers are merge, keep only what is on the account, or cancel, and the
one that discards writes a backup file first.

Signing out leaves everything on the phone exactly as it was.

The app still works with no account. An account buys sync and nothing else,
and no screen is behind a sign-in.
