A privacy policy, and a tag taken back off four campaigns.

The app now has a written privacy policy, linked from About and published at
hasyame.github.io/Thwart/privacy.html, in English and French. It is short,
because there is very little to describe: one permission, two hosts the app
ever talks to, and everything you make staying on your phone. It is needed for
an app store listing, but it is worth having regardless.

The Rise of Red Skull, Sinister Motives, Mutant Genesis and NeXt Evolution no
longer carry a "not fully tested" tag. Fear No Evil keeps its WIP tag, which
means something different: that one really is unfinished.
