A new face for decks, campaigns and the home page, and a draft.

The home page comes first in the bar: what this version changed, a menu
of the things done most, and the links to Patreon, the web app and the
source. Rules stay in the bar; the top bars are dark and centred.

Decks are a shelf of tiles with the hero's art, filed in folders that
sync with the web, with a search that looks through deck names, hero
names or the cards inside. The deck page has three tabs: the cards and
the pool to add from, with a stepper on each; the breakdown; and the
information, where the problems are explained, the packs the deck draws
on are listed with what is missing from your collection, and your notes
live. A card the identity cannot play is flagged as having no synergy.

Draft, from the Play page: each player takes a hero and aspects, then
the table picks cards five at a time from what the collection holds
until every deck is full. Tap a card to take it, hold it to read it.

Campaigns are a shelf too: in progress, finished, and the figures, with
the final villain's art, the status, and every game's result in the
order it was played.

Cards read in the device's language until one is chosen in Settings.
