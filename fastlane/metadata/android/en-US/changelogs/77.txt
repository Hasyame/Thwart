A switch on the Decks page: "Use only cards in my collection".

On, which is how it starts, a new deck offers only heroes from packs you
own, and the editor lists only cards you own. Off, everything is offered,
with the missing packs marked, for a deck built ahead of a purchase or from
a friend's boxes.

The editor's own "Cards in my collection" chip is the same switch, and the
choice is remembered.
