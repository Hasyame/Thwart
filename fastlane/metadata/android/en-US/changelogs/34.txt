Easier to read at the table.

Card titles are now in quotes wherever a setup step names one. A step is read
while hunting through a pile, and a title that runs into the sentence around it
is hard to pick out — worst for the ones that are ordinary words, like an
attachment called Passe-Partout, or the one that starts En Tête face up.
Encounter sets are quoted one by one rather than as a list.

Every campaign now carries a note before you start it, like the one Fear No
Evil had: that it is unofficial and reconstructed for the app, that the box and
its rulebook are still needed to play, and what the app takes off your hands —
the market and The Collection in The Galaxy's Most Wanted, the Horsemen order
and the OVERSEER draw in Age of Apocalypse, the TECH upgrades and delay
counters in The Rise of Red Skull, the Infinity Stones in The Mad Titan's
Shadow.
