Fear No Evil, finished properly.

The campaign could not be won. A job pushed three times ended the whole run,
and two pushes land every game into a pile that shrinks as jobs are settled —
so whichever job was left standing always got there first. Simulated three
thousand times with perfect play, it was lost three thousand times. It now
works the way the book says: a job pushed that far is out of the campaign, the
others carry on, and Kingpin is harder at the end for every one you failed to
reach. That last part never fired at all before — the flag it counted was
written where nothing read it.

Before each game the villains strike two places and the table keeps one, on a
page that shows where every job stands.

Setup now names every settled environment and which way up it goes, instead
of telling you to put out "each resolved scenario's environment" and leaving
you to work out which those were. The draw itself was also wrong: an
environment drawn once was held back from later draws, where the book shuffles
everything still standing every time.

The briefing is now in the order you work through it: the decks to find, the
setup itself, then notes on the subordinate you drew. Each job opens with a
few lines of its own.

Typhoid Mary is followed properly. Two tokens on Disturbed Psyche wins her
over, she is put into play as a campaign ally from then on, and if she ends
up in the victory pile she leaves the decks for good. The questions are only put to
tables that have actually met her.

This campaign's text is French only for the moment; the app says so before you
start it.
