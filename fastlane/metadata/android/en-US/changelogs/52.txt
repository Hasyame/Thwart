The card database is back in the app.

Three recent versions were built without it. Installing one fresh gave you
an app with no cards until you downloaded them from Settings. Upgrading was
unaffected, because the cards were already on your device.

If you installed 1.30.2, 1.31.0 or 1.32.0 on a new device and found it
empty, this is why, and this version fixes it.
