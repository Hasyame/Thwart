NeXt Evolution, and the counters finally work in campaigns.

The campaign is five scenarios in order, Morlock Siege through Stryfe, and all
five have to be won. Losing one costs nothing: set it up and play it again.

It turns on a choice rather than a draw. Six player side schemes, and each
scenario you pick one of those you have not used yet. The encounter card paired
with it joins the deck for the rest of the campaign whether you beat the scheme
or not. Beat it and you keep its environment on the table for every scenario
after. Fail, and that reward is gone for good. The app offers what is still
available and records what you take, because the rules say the table chooses,
and that is the only real decision the campaign gives you.

The log carries the rest: which Marauders were routed, since they leave the
game entirely in the second scenario, how many Morlocks you saved, and how much
damage Hope Summers is carrying, which you can hand back later as damage or as
threat.

Counting health and threat now works in a campaign. The setting said "during a
game" and a campaign scenario is a game, but the counters only ever existed on
the standalone play screen, so switching it on and starting a campaign did
nothing at all. It reads off the villain the campaign is about to put out, so
it works for all eight campaigns.

Contact and the crash report no longer do the same thing. Contact opens an
empty draft, because whatever you want to say you can say, and the device
details it used to paste in had to be scrolled past first. The crash report
attaches a text file instead of pasting the trace into the message, and the
file carries the app version, the Android version and the device alongside it.
Nothing is sent by the app either way: the draft opens and you decide.
