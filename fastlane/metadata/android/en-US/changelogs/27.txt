The Rules tab now works in French.

Not only the explanations — the keywords too. Looking up the word printed on
a French card finds "Riposte X" rather than "retaliate", because that is what
you are holding.

It follows the language the app is set to, not the language of your cards:
the rules are the app talking, so somebody reading the interface in French
gets them in French whichever language their collection is printed in.

There are 247 entries now instead of 143, and they follow rulebook v1.8, so
the English is better too. All of it comes from the bilingual reference guide
compiled by deejimy and released under CC0 — he is credited under the list.
