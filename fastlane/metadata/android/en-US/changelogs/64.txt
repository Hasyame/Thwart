The buttons above the statistics tables fit again.

Adding a fourth order left the row too wide, so the words were squeezed on
a narrow screen. The "Sort tables by" heading has gone, the two rate
buttons are named more briefly, and the row wraps instead of deforming.
The same was done to the measure buttons in the previous release.

Under the hood: the app re-checks its curated pack list on every launch,
and now writes only where something has actually changed instead of
rewriting every pack each time. On a normal launch that is no writes at
all, and the app opens sooner for it.
