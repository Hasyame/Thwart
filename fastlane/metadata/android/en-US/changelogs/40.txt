Mutant Genesis.

Five scenarios in order, Sabretooth through Magneto, and all five have to be
won. Losing one costs nothing: you set it up and play it again.

Each player takes one of the four roles at the first scenario and keeps it for
the campaign. The upgrade you start each later scenario with is dealt by the
app from your own role's set of five, not from a pile the table shares, and an
upgrade that has been in play is out of the campaign for the player who had it.
So the five run down one player at a time, which is what the "use it or lose
it" rule is for.

The app keeps the campaign log. Which of the five campaign side schemes you
beat, and so whether you have earned the next scenario's upgrade. Which Future
Past cards were still in circulation at the end of a game, so the right ones go
back into the encounter deck next time and the ones that reached the victory
display stay gone. Whether Jubilee is with you, which captives you freed and
can shuffle into a deck, and which allies you left behind and cannot use again.
The order the Brotherhood arrive in at the mansion is drawn too.

Expert is the expert campaign: the harder villain stages, and hit points that
carry from one scenario to the next.

Card names in the campaign are the ones printed on the French cards, taken from
the card database rather than translated here, so what the app calls a card is
what you are holding.
