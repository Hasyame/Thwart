Adds a Rules tab: the Rules Reference, searchable and offline, so a keyword
can be settled at the table without a signal. Searching puts the matching
keyword first and the rules that merely mention it after, because looking up
"stun" should answer with STUNNED. English only for now.

Settings now carries two languages instead of one. The card language and the
language of the app itself are separate settings, so French cards can be read
in an English interface or the other way round. The choice is remembered.
