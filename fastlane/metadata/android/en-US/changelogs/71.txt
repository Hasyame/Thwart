One box per campaign, and it tells you where you are.

A campaign put away on a long break used to appear twice, once to carry on
with and once to resume. Now there is one box, and it says "In a long
break". Open it and the briefing leads with what you wrote down: when you
put it away, where you stopped, the hit points left. Start the scenario and
the table comes back as you left it, tracker and clock included.

The box also says what the campaign is waiting for, which is not always a
scenario: Fear No Evil and The Galaxy's Most Wanted both stop between jobs
to ask you something first. Under that, how far through you are, with a bar
counted from what your campaign will actually play rather than from the
scenario list.
