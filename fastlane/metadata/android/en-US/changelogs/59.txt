A backup now brings back everything it should.

Restoring used to hand back your decks and campaigns but quietly forget
two things: the scenarios you had ticked off as missing from your boxes,
and your settings. Your language, theme, table name and tracker choice all
travel with the file now, and so do the scenario exclusions. Older backups
still restore, and older versions of the app can still read a new one.

Under the hood, this release prepares the database for syncing between
devices later on. Deleting something now records that you deleted it,
rather than only removing it, so that a second device can eventually be
told. Nothing about how the app behaves changes, and nothing leaves your
phone: there is no account and no server yet.
