Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. That makes nine campaigns, and every campaign box is in.

Five scenarios in order, Black Widow through Baron Zemo, all five to be won,
and losing one costs nothing.

The app does the deduction for you. Nine evidence cards, three of each kind,
and one of each is sealed in the A.I.M. envelope naming the mole. So every card
you win is one the mole does not have. The app remembers what you have gathered
and shows only what is still unaccounted for, each piece marked with its own
icon so it lines up with the grid in your log. When a category is down to a
single card, that card is the mole's, and nobody had to cross anything out.

What it will not do is name the mole. Which combination sits under which board
member is a grid of icons printed in the campaign log, not written anywhere as
text, and a guessed grid would send you to the wrong accusation. The app
narrows it to three cards and the log turns those into a name.

Secrets on the three board members carry from one scenario to the next, which
is what decides whether one of them turns against you for good. So do the
minions and side schemes left standing, the captives you rescued, the Adaptoids
in play, and the surviving Thunderbolts.

Four campaigns now say plainly that they have not been played through at a
table: The Rise of Red Skull, Sinister Motives, Mutant Genesis and NeXt
Evolution. Everything in them is built and tested, but a real game finds the
mistakes a test suite cannot, so they say so and ask you to report anything
wrong.

Picking a card in a campaign question works properly now. Tapping the card's
name did nothing before, because only the switch at the far edge of the row
was listening, and the name is where a finger goes.
