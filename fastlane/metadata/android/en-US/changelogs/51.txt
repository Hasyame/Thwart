The app now tells you when there are cards you have not got.

It asks MarvelCDB when it opens, and if a pack is missing, or has gained
cards since your last refresh, it offers to fetch them. Say no and it stops
asking about that pack, while a set released later still gets offered.
Nothing is downloaded until you say yes, and nothing is said at all when
there is no connection.

The Cards and Decks tabs also have icons that look like cards now, instead
of a magnifying glass and a list.
