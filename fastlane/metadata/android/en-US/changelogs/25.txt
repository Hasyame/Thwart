A random draw, or a setup you chose yourself, now lands on a setup page
before the game starts: what to fetch out of the boxes — the scenario, its
main scheme, the difficulty, the modular sets that were drawn and who is
playing which aspect — and then the setup printed on the scenario's own main
scheme.

The clock waits for the Play button instead of starting the moment the
scenario is known. Laying a game out takes minutes, and they were being
counted as play time, so every recorded game was longer than it really was.

Also fixes losing what you had just done while a screen was still loading:
an aspect ticked while the hero list loaded, or a search typed while the card
list was being fetched, could be quietly undone.
