Backups can now carry your table photos.

They were the one thing a backup left behind: a reinstall restored your
collection, decks, campaigns and play history, and the pictures were gone.

It is a tick box on the Backup screen, off by default. Left alone, nothing
changes and the photos stay on this device, because a backup file gets
handed to people and dropped into shared drives. Ticked, the backup is an
archive carrying them, and restoring puts them back where they belong.

Older backups still restore exactly as before.
