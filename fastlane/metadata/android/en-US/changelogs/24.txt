Fixes two bugs a player reported from a Galaxy Z Fold 7.

Opening or closing the phone no longer throws you into the settings menu and
leaves the Play tab unable to move. Rotating any phone did the same thing; a
fold just does it every time you open it.

Heroes are no longer missing from the randomiser. Every install pre-ticked
somebody else's fifteen packs, so the app hid the heroes it believed you did
not own. A new install now assumes the Core Set and nothing else, and the
collection screen is there to correct it.

Tapping the tab you are already on now returns to its list. After building a
deck there was no way back to the deck list except the back gesture.

Campaign briefings now print the setup written on the scenario's own main
scheme, after the campaign's setup, in the order you do it at the table.

Campaigns follow your card language. They were always French, whatever you
had chosen, even though the English text was there all along.

Deck legality has been revised against the printed rules: the hero's own
cards must be present in their printed numbers, copies are counted by title
rather than by printing, unique cards count the identity card, and Adam
Warlock's four balanced aspects and single copies are understood. His decks
were always rejected before.
