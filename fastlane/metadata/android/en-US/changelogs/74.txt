Your other device's changes arrive while you are looking at the app.

If you are signed in with sync switched on, Thwart now listens for changes
made elsewhere and picks them up in about a second, instead of waiting until
this phone happened to have a change of its own. It also asks what it missed
whenever you open the app.

It listens only while the app is on screen, and closes the connection the
moment you leave it. No background service, no notification, nothing running
behind a sleeping phone.

Editing a deck is safer with two devices. A tap now means "one more of these"
rather than "make it three", so adding a card on your phone can no longer
undo a change you made on a tablet, and the deck list updates as changes
arrive rather than after your next tap.

The first-time merge question now warns you when it would keep a deck twice.

An account whose address you have not confirmed yet now says so, instead of
a general error that left you guessing.

Nothing changes if you do not use an account: no connection is opened and
nothing leaves the phone.
