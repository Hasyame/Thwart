Setting up your own game is rebuilt: you pick a deck instead of describing a
hero and an aspect, and scenarios and modular sets are searchable rather than a
wall of chips.

Expert now asks which Standard set goes with it, because that is how the box
plays it. The tracker follows suit: a villain printed I, II and III is played I
then II on Standard, II then III on Expert. All three used to be offered.

A game put away for a long break can be picked up again, with your notes and the
clock where you left them.
