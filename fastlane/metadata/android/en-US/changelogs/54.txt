The statistics page led with nine numbers of equal weight, so finding your
win rate meant reading all of them. It now leads with the win rate alone,
large, with the record written underneath and a bar showing the share.

The hero, aspect and scenario lists were sorted by rate, which put a hero
you won with once above one you have played thirty times. They sort by
games played now, and each line carries a bar instead of a row of numbers
crowding the name.
