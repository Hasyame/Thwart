Counters at the table, if you want them.

New setting: track health and threat. Off unless you turn it on, and with it
off nothing about the app changes.

With it on, a game in progress shows the villain's health and the main
scheme's threat, taken from the printed cards and worked out for the number of
people playing — Rhino is 14 solo and 56 at four, and you never have to do
that multiplication. A button ends the round and adds the acceleration, which
is the thing everybody forgets. The screen stays awake while you play, and a
switch on the play screen gives that up for one game if you would rather it
did not.

Counters only. It does not know that a Crisis icon stops thwarting or that a
minion just arrived: a tracker that gets a rule wrong once stops being trusted
for the numbers it had right.

Also: panels that were asked to fill the width used to draw a black slab
beside themselves, and the play screen now scrolls.
