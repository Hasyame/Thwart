The cards are no longer packaged with the app. They are fetched on first
launch instead, about 1.5 MB, and everything works offline after that.

That fixes a real problem: three recent versions were built without the
cards and installed empty, and the F-Droid package never had them at all.
Every build now behaves the same way wherever you got it from. The cards
also belong to MarvelCDB and change when MarvelCDB changes, which is a
poor thing to freeze into an app.

The tabs have icons that look like what they hold: a card, a deck, a fist,
a book and a chart.
