Fixes the losses measure added yesterday.

It printed the wrong number. A hero who had lost half his games was shown
as 33%, because the figure was being divided by everyone else's losses
instead of by that hero's own games. Wins were always right; only losses
were affected, and only since 1.40.2.

The row of buttons above each chart also took too much room once there
were four of them, and squeezed the words on a narrow screen. The
"Showing" label in front of them has gone, and the buttons now wrap
instead of deforming.
