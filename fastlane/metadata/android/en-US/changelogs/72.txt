The campaign box shows the campaign.

A campaign in progress used to be a line: a name, a difficulty, your
heroes. It is a panel now, and it answers without being opened. What the
campaign is waiting for. How long you have played, the game on the clock
included. How far through you are, on a bar that spans the panel.

And every scenario as a chip that says where it stands: the one on the
table, the ones you won, the ones you lost, the ones still to come, and in
Fear No Evil the ones pushed off the board without ever being played,
struck through.

Finished campaigns stay a plain list, so the one you are actually playing
is the one that stands out.
