The draft deals in packs.

Once the table is set, the packs are built in advance from your
collection: one pack per card to take, each of X distinct cards, a copy
you own once in one pack only, a card a deck may hold once in one pack
only. You open them one by one and take a single card from each; the rest
goes back on the shelf. When the shelf runs short, packs are built again
from what came back, always full.

A card you own only as a reprint is now filed under the pack you own, and
the deck page counts any printing you own as owned.
