The French across all nine campaigns, checked page by page against the printed
booklets and against the cards themselves.

Card names that had been left in English are translated, the game's own
vocabulary is used throughout, and setup steps written as shorthand are
sentences again. The Collection now pays its credit the way the booklet counts
it, once for the table rather than hero by hero.

Every campaign page says how much of that campaign is written in French and in
English.
