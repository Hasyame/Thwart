The Fear No Evil finale counts your settled missions for you.

Two of Le Caïd's setup steps used to open with "if three missions or more
are ACHEVÉ", which asks you to count something the campaign log already
knows. They now say what to do and how many you have.

The same declaration decides whether a step appears and fills in its
number, so the two can never disagree, and the campaign files are checked
for it when they load.
