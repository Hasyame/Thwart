The racket job is counted properly, and you choose your difficulty cards.

Fear No Evil deals its racket job a main scheme to every player, and the
tracker only ever counted one. It now counts one each, named for whose it
is.

Threat from pressure is worked out rather than described: the app states
the number for the campaign you are playing instead of printing the rule
for both difficulties. That settles the tanker in La Poursuite, and stops
a standard campaign being told what an expert one does. Four other
campaigns had the same wording and were corrected too.

Starting a campaign now asks which difficulty cards go in the encounter
deck: pick a set, or leave it to the app to draw one you own.
