Fixes and housekeeping, no change to what the app does.

Three pack names that were showing in English now show in French: Agents du
S.H.I.E.L.D., Peur de Rien and La Félonie du Filou. The rest of the names the
card database leaves in English are names the French edition prints in English
too, so they are correct as they stand.

Two things in the BoardGameGeek connection, found by reading the code rather
than from a report. A session cookie could have travelled off BoardGameGeek if
one of its pages had redirected elsewhere; it is now held to the site it
belongs to. And a password containing a tab or a line break built a request
BoardGameGeek could only refuse — you were then told your credentials were
wrong, which was neither true nor something you could do anything about.

The F-Droid listing is in French now, for anyone who finds the app there.
