A sync fix, for accounts also used on the web.

The web can now star a game and rate how hard a scenario was, and the
account then holds kinds of data this app does not read yet. Meeting one
of those, the app used to stop moving forward: every sync re-read the
same page and, once enough had happened after it, never finished at all.
The app now tells the server which kinds of data it reads and is sent
only those, passes anything it cannot read rather than waiting on it, and
reads the whole account again, once, the day a version reads more kinds.
