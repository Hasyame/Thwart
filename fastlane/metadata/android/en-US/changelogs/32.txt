Fear No Evil joins the campaigns.

It is the odd one out, and the app now handles it properly. There is no
scenario order: before every game two environments are drawn and push their
scenarios further along, whether or not anybody goes there, and then the table
picks which one to actually play. A scenario pushed three times is lost
without ever being played — its environment stays on the table for the rest of
the campaign, and Kingpin is harder at the end for every one you failed to
save.

The app keeps the campaign log for you: which villain faced which scenario,
how far each has been pushed, the two campaign questions, the allies and
supports struck off for good, and in an expert campaign each hero's remaining
hit points between games.

One limit worth knowing: MarvelCDB has published only the hero side of this
pack, so the setup steps name cards in words rather than linking them. When
the encounter cards appear, the links follow.
