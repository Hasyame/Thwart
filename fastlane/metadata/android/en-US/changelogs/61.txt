Two small things on the "putting the game away" page.

Your hero is named rather than shown as a card code. The name on the deck
was there all along; the page was looking it up in the wrong place first.

And when the tracker is running, the page no longer asks you to type the
villain's health. The app already has that number on the screen in front
of you, so it keeps it itself, and the summary you see when you pick the
game up again still shows it. With the tracker off, nothing changes: the
fields are still there, because then what you write is the only record.
