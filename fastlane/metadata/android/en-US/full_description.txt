An unofficial, offline companion for Marvel Champions: The Card Game.

Thwart keeps track of the things a game of Marvel Champions asks you to keep
track of, so the table can get on with playing.

<b>Cards</b>
The full card catalogue, searchable offline, accent-insensitive. Filter by type,
aspect, cost and traits; sort by set, name or cost. Star the ones you care
about. Card text is shown in the language you choose, independently of the
language of the app itself.

<b>Your collection</b>
Mark the packs you own and everything else follows from it: the randomiser only
draws what you can put on the table, and decks are checked against it. Owning a
pack is not owning everything in it, so you can untick individual modular sets
that are missing from a second-hand box.

<b>Decks</b>
Import from a decklist link, build from scratch, check legality as you go, and
share a list as plain text. Deck building rules are enforced, including the
heroes who rewrite them: Adam Warlock's four balanced aspects, Spider-Woman's
two, Gamora's off-aspect events.

<b>Rules</b>
The rules reference in a tab of its own, searchable and offline, because the
moment somebody needs to know what retaliate does is the moment the rulebook is
in a bag under the table. In English and French, keywords included.

<b>Random games</b>
Scenario, difficulty, modular sets, heroes and aspects, drawn only from what you
own. Lock anything you have already decided and roll the rest around it. A setup
page then tells you what to fetch out of the boxes, including the setup printed
on the scenario's own main scheme.

<b>Campaigns</b>
Four campaigns ship with the app. Each scenario's setup is worked out from what
you recorded in the ones before it, so the app tells you what to put on the
table rather than making you re-read the book. What it stores is an append-only
log, so a finished campaign is a record of what actually happened.

<b>Play log and statistics</b>
Games are timed and recorded. Win rate by hero, by scenario, by aspect, by
difficulty, and solo against multiplayer, kept apart because a blended figure
describes neither.

<b>No account, no server, no advertising</b>
Everything stays on the device. There is no analytics and no backend. A backup
is a single readable JSON file you control.

<b>Not affiliated with Marvel or Fantasy Flight Games</b>
Thwart is a fan-made play aid. Marvel Champions: The Card Game is a Marvel
property published by Fantasy Flight Games, and this app is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by them. The game is named here to say what the app
works with. Card data is fetched from MarvelCDB; no card images, card text or
campaign book text is stored in this project. You need the game itself, and its
rulebooks, to use any of this.
